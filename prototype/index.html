Glyphs beside a line
solid underline = seen directly. dashed = told, not witnessed. dotted = inferred. no underline = not yet decidable. double underline (flickers) = only decidable now because of something later; the words never changed, only this mark did.
STIPULATED
established, not worth contesting.
SUSTAINED
your claim holds against current evidence.
OVERRULED
your claim conflicts with something stronger.
REDACTED
exists, withheld. the withholding is itself evidence.
CANNOT BE ESTABLISHED
not a verdict on your claim: the machine recognized no term it knows.
Recalibrate
once per session. restores the machine's coherence, but stops the flicker mark for the rest of this session.
Two memories
this session resets on New Session. what the machine has retained about every session it has ever run does not, and lives on this device only.
