Legend

Glyph header positions

ORIGIN
How far into the record this session has gone. It does not measure progress — you can surface and re-descend.
OBSTACLE
How coherent the record currently is. Contradictions wear it down; it can be restored (see Recalibrate below).
OUTCOME
Whether it has been established yet who was last in the room, and if so, who.

Verdict vocabulary

STIPULATED solid border
Established. Not worth contesting further.
DISPUTED dashed border
Active, unresolved contradiction.
SUSTAINED double border
Your theory holds against current evidence.
OVERRULED dotted border
Your theory is incompatible with something stronger.
REDACTED diagonal-hatched fill
Exists, withheld. The withholding itself is evidence.
CANNOT BE ESTABLISHED grooved border, italic
Not a verdict on your claim — the parser's own admission that your wording used no term it recognizes.

Epistemic status glyphs (beside sentences)

● OBSERVED — solid underline
Directly seen by the machine in this session.
○ REPORTED — dashed underline
Told to the machine, not witnessed directly.
◇ INFERRED — dotted underline
Deduced, not stated outright.
· UNRESOLVED — no underline
Not yet decidable from the record.
◆ RETROACTIVE — double underline, gently flickers
Only became decidable because of something that happened later. The sentence's words never changed — only this indicator did.

Recalibrate (maintenance ritual)

Once per session. Restores coherence toward its starting value, but permanently stops the RETROACTIVE glyph from flickering for the rest of this session — you keep a steadier record at the cost of a visible tell. Leaving it alone lets coherence keep drifting down as contradictions accumulate, but keeps that flicker visible.

Two memories

RECORD is this session's world-state. It resets whenever you start a New Session. IMPRESSION is what the machine has retained about every session it has ever run; it is stored on this device and never resets.
